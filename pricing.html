Billing questions

What does "real sources" mean?

In Premium rounds the AI argues from genuine retrieved evidence and gives you the link for each card, so you can open the source, read it in context, and cut it yourself. Free rounds still run the full format, but the AI argues without attached citations.

Will the free tools ever go away?

No. Flowing, casewriting, and the training modules stay free. Premium exists for debaters who run rounds constantly and want sourced evidence.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your Account page, in a couple of clicks. You keep Premium until the end of the period you paid for, then move to the free plan. Rounds and ballots you saved while on Premium stay for 30 days after that, the same as any free round.

How does yearly billing work?

You pay $79 once a year instead of $9.99 a month. That is about what one season costs month to month, and 34% less than paying monthly all year. It renews each year until you cancel.

Who handles the payment?

Stripe. Your card goes straight to them and never touches our servers. Receipts go to the email entered at checkout, so a parent can pay with their own card and email.

Is this allowed in real rounds?

These tools are for practice. Cases and evidence generated here should be verified and cut by you before they go anywhere near a live round.
