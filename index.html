Mission Statement

PublicForumAI is dedicated to making public forum debate more accessible. We provide resources and guidance to help debaters of all levels improve, especially those without access to traditional support. Our goal is to equip students with the skills and confidence to succeed in debate.

About PublicForumAI

Built by debaters

PublicForumAI was started in 2024 by students on a high school debate team in New York City, and was built to cater to the experiences of others like them in Public Forum debate.

Purpose-built for PF

Every tool follows the standard Public Forum structure, from the speech order in a practice round to the way a ballot is written.

What is next

We are working with schools and debate camps to tailor the platform to how programs actually train, and building a mobile app. Your feedback as a debater is what helps us become the best product we can be.
